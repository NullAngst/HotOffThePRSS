Several addresses can share one feed, for example the RSS feeds of four sections of the same forum. They all use this feed's name, cookies, schedule and destinations, and an article that appears in more than one of them is posted once. A newly added source's existing articles are marked as seen, not posted.

Access: cookies and User-Agent{% if feed.cookies %} cookies set{% endif %}

For feeds that only show everything to signed-in members, such as a XenForo forum. Sign in to the site in your browser with Stay logged in ticked, then copy its cookies from the developer tools (Storage or Application, Cookies). For XenForo, xf_user is the one that matters; adding xf_session does no harm. Signing out of the site in that browser invalidates the cookie, so a private window you simply close works best.
